# Workout 4: Legs and forearms
### Saturday session: back and arm volume first (seated row, curls, triceps), then maintenance legs and forearms

| | |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | About 75 min |
| **Equipment** | Pilates bar, tube bands (30, 40, 50 lb), door anchor, loop bands, a chair, a folded towel |
| **Rest between sets** | 45-90 s (single-leg work: rest after both legs) |
| **Tempo (all exercises)** | 3 s lowering, 1 s squeeze at the hardest point, 1 s lifting |
| **Effort** | Most sets end with 1-2 reps left in reserve. The last set of each exercise goes to failure when it is safe |

> **Safety check:** inspect every band for nicks, cracks or thinning before each session, and never use a damaged one. Check that every clip on the bar and the anchor is fully closed. When using the door anchor, the door must be **closed and latched**, and it should open **away from you** so the pull wedges it shut. Pad the bar with a folded towel for squats.

## Bar setups used today

| Setup | How |
|---|---|
| **Door and bar** | Door anchor at the lowest point of the door. A tube runs from the anchor to each end of the bar (matching tubes, see the Program Guide for tube pairing). |
| **Bar under feet** | Lay the bar on the floor and stand on it. A tube on each end, hold the handle at the far end of each tube. |
| **Bar on back, feet in handles** | A tube clipped to each end of the bar. Put a foot in the handle at the other end of each tube. Rest the bar across your upper back. |
| **Bar in hands, feet in handles** | Same tubes, holding the bar in your hands. Stand wider for more tension. |

Matching tubes on both ends matter most here. See the Program Guide for tube pairing.

## Band selection guide

Ratings are the resistance at full stretch, so the real load mid-range is lower. Start here, then adjust so the last 2 reps are hard but clean.

1. **Seated row, light** (30 lb), 1 × 15

## The exercises

### 1. Seated row: tube, Pilates bar and door, 4 × 10-15
**Targets:** mid-back, lats, rear delts, biceps

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **lowest** point of the door, a tube from the anchor to each end of the bar.
- Sit on the floor facing the door with legs extended and knees soft, far enough back that the tubes are taut.
- Hold the bar overhand, shoulder-width, sitting tall with arms extended.

**Execution**
1. Pull the bar to your lower ribs, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
2. Squeeze your shoulder blades together for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s to full arm extension without rounding your back.

**Cues:** "Chest tall, elbows back."

**Mistakes:** rounding the back, leaning back to cheat, shrugging.

**Progress:** sit farther back, stack tubes, or use an underhand grip to bring in more biceps.

### 2. Curl biceps (neutral grip): tube and Pilates bar under feet, 2 × 12-15
**Targets:** biceps, brachialis (arm thickness)

**Setup**
- Stand on the bar with feet shoulder-width apart, a tube on each end with a handle at the far end (30 lb).
- Hold one handle in each hand with palms facing each other, elbows at your sides.

**Execution**
1. Curl both hands toward your shoulders without rotating your wrists.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Elbows stay pinned to your ribs.

**Mistakes:** swinging, elbows drifting forward.

**Progress:** choke up on the tubes, or use heavier tubes.

### 3. Triceps extension: loop, 2 × 12-15
**Targets:** triceps (long head)

**Setup**
- Stand on one end of a loop band (15-50) with your front foot and hold the other end behind your head with both hands, elbows forward.

**Execution**
1. Extend your arms straight up.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Upper arms stay still.

**Mistakes:** elbows flaring, arching the lower back.

**Progress:** next loop up, or single-arm.

### 4. Squat: tube and Pilates bar on back, 3 × 12-20
**Targets:** quads, glutes, hamstrings

**Setup**
- A tube on each end of the bar (50 lb), your feet in the handles at the other ends, feet shoulder-width apart.
- Rest the bar, padded with a folded towel, across your upper back and hold it near the ends.
- Stand tall so the tubes are taut.

**Execution**
1. Sit back and down, knees tracking over your toes, until your thighs are about parallel to the floor.
2. Pause for 1 s at the bottom.
3. Drive through your mid-foot to stand, squeezing your glutes at the top.
4. Lower in 3 s on every rep.

**Cues:** Chest tall, knees out, weight over the middle of your foot.

**Mistakes:** knees caving in, heels lifting, rounding the lower back, leaning too far forward.

**Progress:** stack tubes, shorten the tubes (more tension at the top), or add a 2 s pause at the bottom.

### 5. Romanian deadlift: tube and Pilates bar in hands, 3 × 10-15
**Targets:** hamstrings, glutes, lower back

> *Not on your chart. Added for hamstrings, which nothing else here trains directly.*

**Setup**
- A tube on each end of the bar (40 lb), feet in the handles at the other ends, feet shoulder-width apart.
- Stand tall holding the bar overhand at your thighs, shoulders back, knees soft.

**Execution**
1. Push your hips back and lower the bar along your legs until you feel a strong hamstring stretch (about mid-shin), back flat.
2. Pause for 1 s.
3. Drive your hips forward to stand, squeezing your glutes at the top.
4. Lower in 3 s on every rep.

**Cues:** "Hips back, back flat." Keep the bar close to your legs.

**Mistakes:** rounding the back, squatting instead of hinging, the bar drifting away from your legs.

**Progress:** stack tubes, shorten the tubes, or add a 2 s pause at the bottom.

### 6. Lunges: loop, 2 × 10-12 each leg
**Targets:** quads, glutes, hamstrings

**Setup**
- Step on the middle of a loop band (25-75) with your front foot and hold the top of the loop at shoulder height, elbows down.
- Step back with the other leg into a split stance, feet hip-width apart.

**Execution**
1. Lower your back knee toward the floor in 3 s, front knee over your ankle.
2. Pause for 1 s just above the floor.
3. Drive through your front foot to stand.
4. Do all reps on one leg, then switch. Rest after both legs.

**Cues:** Torso upright, front heel planted.

**Mistakes:** front knee collapsing inward, leaning forward, a step that is too short.

**Progress:** move to the next loop up, or add a 2 s pause at the bottom.

### 7. Ankle plantarflexion: loop, 3 × 15-20 each leg
**Targets:** calves

**Setup**
- Sit on the floor with one leg extended, a loop band (15-50) around the ball of your foot, ends held in both hands with the band taut.

**Execution**
1. Push your foot forward, pointing your toes, until your ankle is fully extended.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s.
4. Do all reps on one leg, then switch.

**Cues:** Move only at the ankle, keep your knee straight.

**Mistakes:** bending the knee, rushing the lowering.

**Progress:** pull the ends closer for more tension, or move to the next loop up.

### 8. Reverse curl: tube and Pilates bar in hands, 3 × 12-15
**Targets:** forearm extensors, brachioradialis, biceps

**Setup**
- A tube on each end of the bar (30 lb), feet in the handles at the other ends, feet shoulder-width apart.
- Grip the bar **palms down**, shoulder-width, elbows at your sides.

**Execution**
1. Curl the bar toward your shoulders, wrists straight.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s to full arm extension.

**Cues:** Keep your wrists in line with your forearms. Elbows pinned.

**Mistakes:** bending the wrists, swinging the torso.

**Progress:** stand wider, shorten the tubes, or use heavier tubes.

### 9. Wrist curl: tube and Pilates bar in hands, 3 × 15-20
**Targets:** forearm flexors

**Setup**
- A tube on each end of the bar (30 lb), feet in the handles at the other ends.
- Sit on a chair, rest your forearms on your thighs with your wrists hanging past your knees. Grip the bar **palms up**, shoulder-width.

**Execution**
1. Curl your wrists up as far as you can.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s to a full wrist stretch.

**Cues:** Only your wrists move.

**Mistakes:** lifting your forearms off your thighs, using momentum.

**Progress:** if 30 lb is too heavy, sit with your feet closer to the chair to reduce the stretch. To go heavier, move your feet farther away.

### 10. Wrist extension: tube and Pilates bar in hands, 3 × 15-20
**Targets:** forearm extensors

**Setup**
- Same position as the Wrist curl, grip the bar **palms down**.

**Execution**
1. Raise the back of your hands as far as you can.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Only your wrists move.

**Mistakes:** lifting your forearms off your thighs, using momentum.

**Progress:** same as the Wrist curl.
