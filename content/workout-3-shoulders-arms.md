# 🎯 Workout 3: Shoulders and Arms
### Shoulder width, rear delts, arm size, extra back and chest volume

| | |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | ~60 min |
| **Equipment** | Tube bands (30 / 40 / 50 lb) with handles, Pilates bar and door anchor; loop bands |
| **Rest between sets** | 60-90 s (supersets: rest after each pair) |
| **Tempo (all exercises)** | 3 s lowering, 1 s squeeze at the hardest point, 1 s lifting |
| **Effort** | Most sets end with 1-2 reps left in reserve; the last set of each exercise goes to failure when safe |

> **Safety check:** inspect every band for nicks, cracks or thinning before each session, and never use a damaged one. When using the door anchor, the door must be **closed and latched**, and it should open **away from you** so the band's pull wedges it shut. See the Program Guide for how to progress.

## 🎚 Band selection guide

Ratings are the resistance at **full stretch**, so the real load mid-range is lower. Start here, then adjust so the last 2 reps are hard but clean.

| Exercise | Start with |
|---|---|
| Lateral raise, Bend over fly | Loop 5-30 or tube 30 lb |
| Upright row | Loop 25-75 |
| Pull down, Cable crossover | Tube 40-50 lb |
| Wide grip barbell, Side pull biceps | Tube 30-40 lb |
| Push-down tricep | Tube 30-40 lb |
| Triceps extension, Bicep curls | Loop 15-50 |

## 💪 The exercises

### 1. Lateral raise: Loop · 4 × 15-20
**Targets:** side delts (the main driver of shoulder width)

**Setup**
- Stand on the middle of a loop band (5-30, then 15-50) with feet together or slightly apart.
- Hold the band at your sides, palms facing in, slight elbow bend.

**Execution**
1. Raise both arms out to the sides, leading with your elbows, until they reach shoulder height.
2. Hold for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Key cues:** Imagine pouring water out of a jug at the top. Don't swing.

**Common mistakes:** shrugging, momentum from the torso, raising above shoulder height.

**Progress:** next loop up, stand wider, or finish the last set with partial reps in the top half.

---

### 2. Bend over fly: Tube · 3 × 15-20
**Targets:** rear delts, upper back

**Setup**
- Stand on the middle of the tube (30 lb) with feet shoulder-width apart.
- Hinge forward at the hips to about 45°, back flat, arms hanging with a slight elbow bend.

**Execution**
1. Raise both arms out to the sides, leading with your elbows, until they reach shoulder height.
2. Squeeze your rear delts for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Key cues:** Spread your arms apart rather than lifting them.

**Common mistakes:** standing up during the rep, shrugging, pulling with the hands.

**Progress:** choke up on the tube or stack tubes.

---

### 3. Upright row: Loop · 3 × 12-15
**Targets:** side delts, upper traps

**Setup**
- Stand on the middle of a loop band (25-75) with feet shoulder-width apart.
- Hold the top of the loop with hands about shoulder-width apart, arms down.

**Execution**
1. Pull your hands up along your body toward your chest, leading with your elbows.
2. Stop when your elbows reach shoulder height. Don't go higher.
3. Hold for 1 s.
4. Lower in 3 s.

**Key cues:** Elbows stay higher than your wrists. Keep your shoulders down and away from your ears.

**Common mistakes:** pulling the elbows above shoulder height (this can irritate the shoulder), using a very narrow grip, shrugging.

**Progress:** next loop up, or widen your stance.

---

### 4. Pull down (underhand): Tube · 3 × 12-15
**Targets:** lower lats, biceps

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, with the tube and the Pilates bar (or two handles).
- Kneel or stand facing the door, hands palms up about shoulder-width apart, arms overhead and the band taut.

**Execution**
1. Pull the bar down to your upper chest, elbows close to your body.
2. Squeeze your lats and biceps for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s to a full stretch.

**Key cues:** "Elbows to hips."

**Common mistakes:** leaning too far back, shrugging.

**Progress:** step back or stack tubes.

---

### 5. Cable crossover: Tube · 3 × 12-15
**Targets:** chest (lower and mid), front delts

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, with the tube and two handles.
- Face away from the door in a staggered stance, hinge slightly at the hips, with arms out to your sides slightly above shoulder height and a slight elbow bend.

**Execution**
1. Sweep both hands down and across in an arc until they meet in front of your hips or lower chest.
2. Squeeze your chest for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s with your elbow bend unchanged.

**Key cues:** "Hug a big tree downward." Keep your elbow bend constant.

**Common mistakes:** pressing instead of sweeping, shrugging, standing up straight.

**Progress:** step forward or stack tubes.

---

### 6. Superset A: Wide grip barbell + Push-down tricep
Do 10-12 reps of the first, then go straight into 12-15 reps of the second. Rest 60-90 s after the pair. Repeat for 3 rounds.

#### 6a. Wide grip barbell: Tube + Pilates bar
**Targets:** biceps

> *On your chart this is "Wide grip barbell". I've used it as a wide-grip curl with the Pilates bar.*

**Setup**
- Clip the tube(s) to the Pilates bar and stand on the middle of the tube(s).
- Grip the bar palms up, slightly wider than shoulder width, elbows at your sides.

**Execution**
1. Curl the bar toward your shoulders.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Common mistakes:** swinging, elbows drifting forward.

#### 6b. Push-down tricep: Tube
**Targets:** triceps

**Setup**
- Move the door anchor to the **top** of the door, with the tube and handles. Stand facing the door, elbows pinned at about 90°.

**Execution**
1. Push your hands down until your arms are straight.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s.

**Common mistakes:** elbows drifting forward, leaning over.

---

### 7. Superset B: Side pull biceps + Triceps extension
Do 12-15 reps per side of the first, then 12-15 reps of the second. Rest 60-90 s after the pair. Repeat for 3 rounds.

#### 7a. Side pull biceps: Tube
**Targets:** biceps (peak)

> *On your chart this is "Side pull biceps". I've used it as a single-arm curl with the arm out to the side, like a cable biceps pose.*

**Setup**
- Door anchor at about **shoulder height**, with the tube and one handle.
- Stand side-on to the door, working arm extended out to the side at shoulder height, palm up, band taut.

**Execution**
1. Curl the handle toward your head, keeping your upper arm at shoulder height.
2. Squeeze your biceps for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s to a straight arm.

**Common mistakes:** dropping the elbow, shrugging.

#### 7b. Triceps extension: Loop
**Targets:** triceps (long head)

**Setup**
- Stand on one end of a loop band (15-50) with your front foot and hold the other end behind your head with both hands, elbows forward.

**Execution**
1. Extend your arms straight up.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Common mistakes:** elbows flaring, arching the lower back.

---

### 8. Bicep curls (finisher, optional): Loop · 2 × 20
**Targets:** biceps (pump)

**Setup**
- Stand on one end of a loop band (15-50) and hold the other end with both hands, palms up, elbows at your sides.

**Execution**
1. Curl your hands toward your shoulders.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Note:** drop this if you're running past 60 minutes.
