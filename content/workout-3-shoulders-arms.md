# Workout 3: Shoulders and arms
### Priority day: arm size, rear delts and extra lat work, with side delts at maintenance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | About 65 min (about 70 with the optional finisher) |
| **Equipment** | Pilates bar, tube bands (30, 40, 50 lb), door anchor, handles, loop bands |
| **Rest between sets** | 45-75 s (see the overview table) |
| **Tempo (all exercises)** | 3 s lowering, 1 s squeeze at the hardest point, 1 s lifting |
| **Effort** | Most sets end with 1-2 reps left in reserve. The last set of each exercise goes to failure when it is safe |

> **Safety check:** inspect every band for nicks, cracks or thinning before each session, and never use a damaged one. Check that every clip on the bar and the anchor is fully closed. When using the door anchor, the door must be **closed and latched**, and it should open **away from you** so the pull wedges it shut.

## Bar setups used today

| Setup | How |
|---|---|
| **Door and bar** | Door anchor on the door. A tube runs from the anchor to each end of the bar (matching tubes, see the Program Guide for tube pairing). |
| **Bar in hands, feet in handles** | A tube clipped to each end of the bar. Put a foot in the handle at the other end of each tube. Stand wider for more tension. |
| **Bar under feet** | Lay the bar on the floor and stand on it. A tube on each end, hold the handle at the far end of each tube. |

## Band selection guide

Ratings are the resistance at full stretch, so the real load mid-range is lower. Start here, then adjust so the last 2 reps are hard but clean.

## Warm-up (8 min)

1. **Arm circles**, 10 forward and 10 backward
2. **Lateral raise, very light**, 1 × 15
3. **Scapular push-ups**, 1 × 10
4. **Bend over fly, light**, 1 × 15
5. **Pull down, light**, 1 × 12

## The exercises

### 1. Lateral raise: loop, 3 × 15-20
**Targets:** side delts (the main driver of shoulder width)

**Setup**
- Stand on the middle of a loop band (5-30, then 15-50), feet together or slightly apart.
- Hold the band at your sides, palms facing in, slight elbow bend.

**Execution**
1. Raise both arms out to the sides, leading with your elbows, until they reach shoulder height.
2. Hold for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Imagine pouring water out of a jug at the top. Don't swing.

**Mistakes:** shrugging, momentum from the torso, raising above shoulder height.

**Progress:** move to the next loop, stand wider, or finish the last set with partial reps in the top half.

### 2. Bend over fly: tube and Pilates bar under feet, 3 × 15-20
**Targets:** rear delts, mid-back

> *Same crossed setup as the Reverse fly, but with a deeper bend over.*

**Setup**
- Stand on the bar with feet shoulder-width apart, a tube on each end with a handle at the far end (30 lb).
- **Cross the tubes:** left end to your right hand, right end to your left hand.
- Hinge forward until your torso is at about 60° (closer to the floor than in the Reverse fly), back flat, arms hanging with a slight elbow bend.

**Execution**
1. Raise both arms out to the sides, leading with your elbows, until they reach shoulder height.
2. Squeeze your rear delts for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Spread your arms apart.

**Mistakes:** standing up during the rep, shrugging, pulling with the hands.

**Progress:** choke up on the tubes or use heavier tubes.

### 3. Close grip lift: tube and Pilates bar in hands, 4 × 12-15
**Targets:** side delts, upper traps, biceps

**Setup**
- A tube on each end of the bar (30-40 lb), with your feet in the handles at the other ends, feet shoulder-width apart.
- Hold the bar overhand with hands near the middle (about 10-15 cm apart), arms down.

**Execution**
1. Raise the bar up in front of you to head level, elbows going out to the sides, until your hands are in front of your nose.
2. Hold for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Elbows stay higher than your wrists. Keep your shoulders down, away from your ears.

**Mistakes:** swinging the bar with your hips, shrugging, rushing the lowering.

**Progress:** stand wider, shorten the tubes, or use heavier tubes. If you feel pinching at the top of the shoulder, stop at chin height instead.

### 4. Pull down (underhand): tube, Pilates bar and door, 3 × 12-15
**Targets:** lower lats, biceps

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, a tube from the anchor to each end of the bar (40 lb).
- Kneel (or stand) facing the door, bar held **palms up**, hands shoulder-width apart, arms overhead and tubes taut.

**Execution**
1. Pull the bar down to your upper chest, elbows close to your body.
2. Squeeze your lats and biceps for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s to a full stretch.

**Cues:** "Elbows to hips."

**Mistakes:** leaning too far back, shrugging.

**Progress:** step back or stack tubes.

### 5. Push down (straight-arm): tube, Pilates bar and door, 3 × 15
**Targets:** lats (isolation)

> *On your chart this is "Push down". I use it as the straight-arm lat push down.*

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, a tube from the anchor to each end of the bar (30 lb).
- Face the door, step back until taut, and hinge slightly at the hips (about 20°).
- Hold the bar overhand, shoulder-width, in front of you at head height, arms straight with a slight elbow bend.

**Execution**
1. Sweep the bar down in an arc until it reaches your thighs, arms straight.
2. Squeeze your lats hard for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s.

**Cues:** Keep your elbow angle fixed the whole time.

**Mistakes:** bending the elbows (this turns it into a triceps push down), swinging from the hips.

**Progress:** step back or stack tubes.

### 6. Wide grip barbell: tube and Pilates bar in hands, 3 × 10-12
**Targets:** biceps

> *On your chart this is "Wide grip barbell". I use it as a wide-grip curl with the Pilates bar.*

**Setup**
- A tube on each end of the bar (30 lb), feet in the handles at the other ends.
- Grip the bar palms up, slightly wider than shoulder-width, elbows at your sides.

**Execution**
1. Curl the bar toward your shoulders.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Elbows pinned to your ribs.

**Mistakes:** swinging, elbows drifting forward.

**Progress:** stand wider or use heavier tubes.

### 7. Side pull biceps: tube and handle on door, 2 × 12-15 each arm
**Targets:** biceps (peak)

> *On your chart this is "Side pull biceps". I use it as a single-arm curl with the arm out to the side.*

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, a tube with one handle (30 lb).
- Stand side-on to the door, working arm extended out to the side at shoulder height, palm up, tube taut.

**Execution**
1. Curl the handle toward your head, keeping your upper arm at shoulder height.
2. Squeeze your biceps for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s to a straight arm.
4. Do all reps on one arm, then switch.

**Cues:** Keep the elbow high.

**Mistakes:** dropping the elbow, shrugging.

**Progress:** step away from the door or use a heavier tube.

### 8. Push-down tricep: tube and handles on door, 4 × 12-15
**Targets:** triceps

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, a tube with a handle in each hand (30 lb), facing the door, elbows pinned at about 90°.

**Execution**
1. Push your hands down until your arms are straight.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s to elbows at about 90°.

**Cues:** Only your forearms move.

**Mistakes:** elbows drifting forward, leaning over.

**Progress:** step back or stack tubes.

### 9. Triceps extension: loop, 3 × 12-15
**Targets:** triceps (long head)

**Setup**
- Stand on one end of a loop band (15-50) with your front foot and hold the other end behind your head with both hands, elbows forward.

**Execution**
1. Extend your arms straight up.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Upper arms stay still.

**Mistakes:** elbows flaring, arching the lower back.

**Progress:** next loop up, or single-arm.

### 10. Bicep curls (finisher, optional): loop, 2 × 20
**Targets:** biceps (pump)

**Setup**
- Stand on one end of a loop band (15-50) and hold the other end with both hands, palms up, elbows at your sides.

**Execution**
1. Curl your hands toward your shoulders.
2. Squeeze for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Note:** drop this if you are running past 70 minutes or your elbows are sore.
