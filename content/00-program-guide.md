# 📘 Program Guide
### How the 3 workouts fit together, and how to make them actually grow your arms, shoulders and back

---

## 🎯 1. Realistic expectations

You asked for arms that are double their current size (30 cm → 60 cm). That is not achievable, and it's better to know it now than after six months:

- A 60 cm arm is larger than the arms of most professional bodybuilders, who use drugs and train for a decade or more.
- Natural muscle gain is front-loaded: published estimates for an untrained young man range from roughly **4-7 kg of lean mass in a well-executed first year** (conservative) up to ~9-11 kg (McDonald model), and the rate **roughly halves each following year**.
- That is whole-body muscle. The arms get only a fraction of it, so a realistic arm target is roughly **+2-4 cm in year one** and about **+5-8 cm over 2-3 years** (my estimate from these rates, not a measured figure).
- Shoulders and back respond visibly too. Broader delts and a thicker back change how your upper body looks more than arm circumference alone.

**Do bands work?** Yes. A 2019 meta-analysis found that elastic resistance training gives strength gains similar to training with weights and machines (no meaningful difference). Bands do have a ceiling, though: your heaviest setup is about 120 lb (all three tubes stacked) or a 145 lb loop. If you stall for several weeks on everything below, a pair of adjustable dumbbells or a pull-up bar is the cheapest upgrade.

## 📅 2. Weekly layout

Train on non-consecutive days, for example **Mon / Wed / Fri** (or Tue / Thu / Sat).

| Day | Workout | Main focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Workout 1: Pull** | Back, rear delts, biceps |
| 2 | **Workout 2: Push** | Chest, shoulders, triceps |
| 3 | **Workout 3: Shoulders + Arms** | Side delts, arms, extra back and chest |

**Weekly hard sets per muscle** (the range generally linked to good hypertrophy is about 10-20 per week):

| Muscle | Sets / week |
|---|---|
| Back (lats, mid-back) | ~17 |
| Rear delts | ~9 |
| Side delts | ~12 + upright row |
| Chest | ~11 |
| Biceps | ~13 (+ indirect from back work) |
| Triceps | ~16 (+ indirect from pressing) |

Triceps make up most of your upper-arm size, so they get real volume here.

## 📈 3. Progressive overload with bands

You can't add a 2 kg plate, so you progress by making the band harder or the set longer. Use this order:

1. **Add reps** until you can do the top of the range (or 20+ reps for the 15-20 rep exercises) with 1-2 reps left in reserve.
2. **Slow the lowering** to 4 s, and add a 2 s pause at the hardest point.
3. **Increase tension**, in this order: step back / stand wider, choke up on the band, move to the next band, stack tubes.
4. **Go single-arm** on rows, pulldowns and presses when you run out of band.

**Tube ladder** (ratings are at full stretch, so real load at mid-range is lower):

| Step | Setup |
|---|---|
| 1 | 30 lb |
| 2 | 40 lb |
| 3 | 50 lb |
| 4 | 30 + 40 |
| 5 | 40 + 50 |
| 6 | 30 + 40 + 50 |

**Loop ladder:** 5-30 → 15-50 → 25-75 → 35-95 → 50-145.

> **Stacking tubes:** only stack if the clips and handles are rated for it and every tube is undamaged.

**Effort rule:** most sets finish with 1-2 reps left in reserve. The last set of each exercise goes to failure when it is safe to do so (curls, raises, pushdowns, rows).

**Deload:** every 6-8 weeks, do one week at half the sets with the same bands.

---

## 🍽 4. Eating and recovery

- **Calories:** a small surplus (about +250-500 kcal/day above maintenance) supports muscle gain. Eating at maintenance or below limits growth.
- **Protein:** around 1.6-2.2 g per kg of body weight per day, spread over 3-4 meals.
- **Sleep:** aim for 7-9 hours.
- **Gaining weight on the scale** (about 0.25-0.5 kg per month at first) is a good sign, not a problem.

## 📏 5. Measuring progress

Measure every **4 weeks**, at the same time of day, relaxed (not flexed):

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Upper arm | Midway between shoulder tip and elbow, arm hanging relaxed, tape snug but not tight |
| Shoulders | Around the widest point of the shoulders, over the deltoids |
| Chest | Around the nipple line, after a normal exhale |
| Body weight | Morning, same conditions each time |
| Photos | Front, side, back, same light and distance |
