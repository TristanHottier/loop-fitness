# 🏋️ Workout 2: Push Day
### Chest, shoulders, triceps

| | |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | ~60 min |
| **Equipment** | Tube bands (30 / 40 / 50 lb) with handles, Pilates bar and door anchor; loop bands |
| **Rest between sets** | 60-90 s |
| **Tempo (all exercises)** | 3 s lowering, 1 s squeeze at the hardest point, 1 s pressing |
| **Effort** | Most sets end with 1-2 reps left in reserve; the last set of each exercise goes to failure when safe |

> **Safety check:** inspect every band for nicks, cracks or thinning before each session, and never use a damaged one. When using the door anchor, the door must be **closed and latched**, and it should open **away from you** so the band's pull wedges it shut. See the Program Guide for how to progress.

## 🎚 Band selection guide

Ratings are the resistance at **full stretch**, so the real load mid-range is lower. Start here, then adjust so the last 2 reps are hard but clean.

| Exercise | Start with |
|---|---|
| Lying chest press | Loop 25-75 or 35-95 |
| Hex press | Tube 40-50 lb |
| Standing press | Tube 40 lb |
| Lateral raises | Tube 30 lb |
| Push-down tricep | Tube 30-40 lb |
| Triceps extension | Loop 15-50 |

## 💪 The exercises

### 1. Lying chest press: Loop · 4 × 8-15
**Targets:** chest, front delts, triceps

**Setup**
- Lie on your back on the floor with your knees bent.
- Wrap a loop band (25-75 or 35-95) across your upper back, under your shoulder blades.
- Hold each side of the band at chest level, elbows at about 45° from your torso.

**Execution**
1. Press your hands straight up until your arms are fully extended (the band is hardest here).
2. Squeeze your chest for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s until your upper arms lightly touch the floor.

**Key cues:** Keep your shoulder blades pinched and flat on the floor. Push the ceiling away.

**Common mistakes:** the band slipping up to your neck (keep it across the shoulder blades), flaring the elbows to 90°, bouncing off the floor.

**Progress:** heavier loop, double loop, or add a 2 s pause at the top.

---

### 2. Hex press: Tube · 4 × 12-15
**Targets:** inner chest, triceps

> *On your chart this is "Hex press". I've used it as a squeeze press with the handles pressed together.*

**Setup**
- Door anchor at **chest height**, with the tube and two handles.
- Face away from the door, hold the handles together at chest level with palms facing each other, and step forward into a staggered stance until taut.

**Execution**
1. Press the handles straight forward, **keeping them pressed together** the whole way.
2. Squeeze your chest for 1 s at full extension.
3. Return in 3 s with the handles still touching.

**Key cues:** Squeeze the handles into each other as if crushing a ball.

**Common mistakes:** letting the handles drift apart, rounding the shoulders at the end.

**Progress:** step farther forward, or stack tubes.

---

### 3. Standing press: Tube · 4 × 10-12
**Targets:** shoulders (front and side delts), triceps

**Setup**
- Stand on the middle of the tube (40 lb) with feet shoulder-width apart.
- Hold the handles at shoulder height, palms facing forward, elbows slightly in front of your body.

**Execution**
1. Squeeze your glutes and brace your abs so your ribs stay down.
2. Press your hands straight up until your arms are nearly locked out.
3. Hold for 1 s.
4. Lower in 3 s back to shoulder height.

**Key cues:** Move your head slightly forward at the top.

**Common mistakes:** arching the lower back, pressing the band out in front.

**Progress:** choke up on the tube, stack tubes, or go single-arm.

---

### 4. Lateral raises: Tube · 4 × 15-20
**Targets:** side delts (shoulder width)

**Setup**
- Stand on the middle of the tube (30 lb), feet together or slightly apart.
- Hold the handles at your sides, palms facing in, slight elbow bend.

**Execution**
1. Raise both arms out to the sides, leading with your elbows, until they reach shoulder height.
2. Hold for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Key cues:** Imagine pouring water out of a jug at the top. Don't swing.

**Common mistakes:** shrugging, raising above shoulder height, using momentum.

**Progress:** stand with feet wider, choke up, or stack tubes. Finish the last set with partial reps in the top half.

---

### 5. Push-down tricep: Tube · 4 × 12-15
**Targets:** triceps

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, with the tube and the handles (or the Pilates bar).
- Face the door with elbows at your sides, bent to about 90°.

**Execution**
1. Pin your elbows to your ribs.
2. Push your hands down until your arms are straight.
3. Squeeze your triceps for 1 s.
4. Return in 3 s to elbows at about 90°.

**Key cues:** Only your forearms move.

**Common mistakes:** elbows drifting forward or flaring, leaning over the band.

**Progress:** stack tubes or step back.

---

### 6. Triceps extension: Loop · 3 × 12-15
**Targets:** triceps (long head)

**Setup**
- Stand on one end of a loop band (15-50) with your front foot and hold the other end behind your head with both hands.
- Elbows point forward, upper arms close to your ears.

**Execution**
1. Extend your arms straight up.
2. Squeeze your triceps for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s until your hands are behind your head.

**Key cues:** Only your forearms move.

**Common mistakes:** elbows flaring out, arching the lower back.

**Progress:** next loop up, or go single-arm.
