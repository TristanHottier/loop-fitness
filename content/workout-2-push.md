# Workout 2: Push day
### Triceps (priority), with chest, shoulders and abs at maintenance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | About 55 min |
| **Equipment** | Pilates bar, tube bands (30, 40, 50 lb), door anchor, handles, loop bands |
| **Rest between sets** | 60-90 s (see the overview table) |
| **Tempo (all exercises)** | 3 s lowering, 1 s squeeze at the hardest point, 1 s pressing |
| **Effort** | Most sets end with 1-2 reps left in reserve. The last set of each exercise goes to failure when it is safe |

> **Safety check:** inspect every band for nicks, cracks or thinning before each session, and never use a damaged one. Check that every clip on the bar and the anchor is fully closed. When using the door anchor, the door must be **closed and latched**, and it should open **away from you** so the pull wedges it shut.

## Bar setups used today

| Setup | How |
|---|---|
| **Door and bar** | Door anchor on the door. A tube runs from the anchor to each end of the bar (matching tubes, see the Program Guide for tube pairing). |
| **Bar under feet** | Lay the bar on the floor and stand on it. A tube on each end, hold the handle at the far end of each tube. |
| **Bar on chest, tube under you** | One tube clipped between both ends of the bar, passing under your upper back as you lie on the floor. |

## Warm-up (8 min)

1. **Arm circles**, 10 forward and 10 backward
2. **Scapular push-ups** (plank position, shoulder blades only), 2 × 10
3. **Push-ups, no band**, 1 × 10
4. **Lateral raises, light**, 1 × 15
5. **Grip press, light**, 1 × 12

## The exercises

### 1. Grip press: tube and Pilates bar, 3 × 8-15
**Targets:** chest, front delts, triceps

**Setup**
- Clip one tube from one end of the bar to the other (handles removed or tucked away).
- Lie on your back on the floor with knees bent and feet flat. Pass the tube **under your upper back** and hold the bar on your chest.
- Hands overhand, slightly wider than shoulder-width.

**Execution**
1. Press the bar straight up above your chest until your arms are fully extended.
2. Squeeze your chest for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s until the bar lightly touches your chest.

**Cues:** Keep your shoulder blades pinched and flat on the floor. Push the ceiling away.

**Mistakes:** the tube sliding up toward your neck (keep it across the upper back), elbows flaring to 90°, the bar drifting toward your face.

**Progress:** add or swap in a heavier tube, or add a 2 s pause at the top. When the tubes are no longer enough, use the Lying chest press with a heavy loop (35-95 or 50-145): lie down, loop across your upper back, press the sides up.

### 2. Hex press: tube and Pilates bar under feet, 2 × 12-15
**Targets:** inner and upper chest, front delts, triceps

**Setup**
- Stand on the bar with feet shoulder-width apart, a tube on each end with a handle at the far end (30 lb).
- Hold the handles **together** at chest height, palms facing each other, elbows pointing down.

**Execution**
1. Press your hands up in front of you until your arms are almost straight, keeping the handles pressed together the whole way.
2. Squeeze your chest for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s with the handles still touching.

**Cues:** Squeeze the handles into each other as if crushing a ball.

**Mistakes:** letting the handles drift apart, leaning back, shrugging.

**Progress:** use heavier tubes or choke up on the tubes.

### 3. Standing press: tube, Pilates bar and door, 3 × 10-12
**Targets:** front delts, upper chest, triceps

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, a tube from the anchor to each end of the bar (40 lb).
- Face away from the door and hold the bar at chest and shoulder height with a **wide grip**.
- Step forward into a staggered stance until the tubes are taut.

**Execution**
1. Brace your abs and squeeze your glutes. Lean slightly forward, because the tubes pull you up and back.
2. Press the bar forward, away from you and the door, until your arms are straight.
3. Hold for 1 s.
4. Return in 3 s to chest height.

**Cues:** "Push the bar through the wall."

**Mistakes:** leaning back, elbows flaring to 90°, shrugging.

**Progress:** step farther forward, stack tubes, or use a narrower stance.

### 4. Lateral raises: tube and Pilates bar under feet, 3 × 15-20
**Targets:** side delts (shoulder width)

**Setup**
- Stand on the bar with feet together or slightly apart, a tube on each end with a handle at the far end (30 lb). Do not cross the tubes.
- Hold the handles at your sides, palms facing in, slight elbow bend.

**Execution**
1. Raise both arms out to the sides, leading with your elbows, until they reach shoulder height.
2. Hold for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s.

**Cues:** Imagine pouring water out of a jug at the top. Don't swing.

**Mistakes:** shrugging, raising above shoulder height, using momentum.

**Progress:** use heavier tubes, choke up, or do the last set with partial reps in the top half.

### 5. Push-down tricep: tube, Pilates bar and door, 4 × 12-15
**Targets:** triceps

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, a tube from the anchor to each end of the bar (30-40 lb).
- Face the door, hold the bar overhand, shoulder-width, elbows at your sides bent to about 90°.

**Execution**
1. Pin your elbows to your ribs.
2. Push the bar down until your arms are straight.
3. Squeeze your triceps for 1 s.
4. Return in 3 s to elbows at about 90°.

**Cues:** Only your forearms move.

**Mistakes:** elbows drifting forward or flaring, leaning over the bar.

**Progress:** step back or stack tubes.

### 6. Triceps extension: loop, 4 × 12-15
**Targets:** triceps (long head)

**Setup**
- Stand on one end of a loop band (15-50) with your front foot and hold the other end behind your head with both hands.
- Elbows point forward, upper arms close to your ears.

**Execution**
1. Extend your arms straight up.
2. Squeeze your triceps for 1 s.
3. Lower in 3 s until your hands are behind your head.

**Cues:** Only your forearms move.

**Mistakes:** elbows flaring out, arching the lower back.

**Progress:** move to the next loop up, or go single-arm.

### 7. Kneeling crunch: tube and handles on door, 3 × 12-15
**Targets:** abs

> *On your chart this is "Kneeling crunch". I use it as a kneeling cable-style crunch.*

**Setup**
- Door anchor at the **top** of the door, a tube with a handle in each hand (40 lb).
- Kneel on a mat facing the door, about 1 m away. Hold the handles beside your head, elbows pointing down, hips stacked over your knees.

**Execution**
1. Round your spine and curl your ribs toward your hips, bringing your elbows toward your thighs.
2. Squeeze your abs for 1 s.
3. Return in 3 s, keeping your hips still.

**Cues:** "Ribs to hips."

**Mistakes:** sitting back on your heels, hinging at the hips instead of curling the spine, pulling with the arms.

**Progress:** use a heavier tube or step back.

- **Overhead triceps stretch:** 30 s each side
